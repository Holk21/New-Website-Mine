About the server

Aotearoa Nationwide RP is built around grounded, low-drama roleplay set across a fictional stretch of New Zealand. We run whitelisted emergency services, a custom economy, and a staff team that actually reads reports.

New here? Start with the rules, then apply to whichever department fits — civilian life needs no application, just connect and go.

Server rules

Every member and applicant is expected to know these before they connect.

General conduct Roleplay standards Combat and death Vehicles and driving Communication Emergency services

General conduct

  1. Treat every member with respect, in and out of character. Harassment, hate speech, and discrimination result in an immediate ban.
  2. No cheating, exploiting bugs, or using unauthorised third-party software. Report bugs instead of abusing them.
  3. Do not share other members' personal information without consent.
  4. Staff decisions are final in the moment. If you disagree, open a ticket rather than arguing in chat.

Roleplay standards

  1. Stay in character while connected, except in designated OOC channels.
  2. No metagaming — using information your character couldn't know in-character.
  3. No powergaming — forcing actions on another player without giving them a chance to react.
  4. Roleplay injuries, restraint, and fear realistically. Your character should value their life.
  5. Fail RP (refusing to roleplay a scenario properly, such as ignoring a robbery) is not permitted.

Combat and death

  1. New Life Rule applies: after being revived from a critical injury, your character forgets the event that caused it.
  2. Random death match — attacking without roleplay justification — is not allowed.
  3. Combat logging (disconnecting to avoid a scenario) will result in a ban.
  4. Value your character's life during hostage or robbery situations; unrealistic risk-taking may be treated as fail RP.

Vehicles and driving

  1. Drive to the standard of the vehicle and situation — reckless driving without RP reason is not allowed.
  2. No fail-spawning, deliberate desync abuse, or vehicle glitching.
  3. Ramming or using vehicles as weapons requires clear roleplay justification.

Communication

  1. Keep OOC chat and Discord respectful. No advertising other servers.
  2. Use in-character voice proximity for in-character conversations — no OOC information over IC channels.
  3. Report rule breaks through a staff ticket with evidence rather than public callouts.

Emergency services

  1. Members of police, fire, and ambulance must complete their department's onboarding before active duty.
  2. Use of department vehicles, equipment, or authority outside of shift is not permitted.
  3. Emergency responders roleplay procedure realistically — corners can be cut for pacing, not skipped entirely.
  4. Abuse of in-character authority (corruption without an approved storyline, excessive force) is handled by department leadership and server staff.
